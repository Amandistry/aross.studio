Donor & Partner Introduction · 2026

Presentation Design | Nonprofit Communications | Information Design

A 10-slide corporate presentation designed to introduce a nonprofit organization to potential donors and community partners. The presentation uses cohesive branding, typography, infographics, data visualization, and visual storytelling to communicate the organization’s mission, programs, impact, and partnership opportunities.

Key figures are set as KPI cards rather than buried in paragraphs, growth is shown as a single ascending bar chart, and the foundation’s navy, teal, and gold identity carries through all 10 slides with consistent slide numbering and a running footer.
