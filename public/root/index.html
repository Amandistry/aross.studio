About

I’m a graphic designer working in print, branding, and digital. My background spans screen printing production, association communications, and freelance work for small businesses and non-profits — which means I’m as comfortable preparing a file for a press as I am building a campaign for social.

I hold an Associate of Arts in Graphic Design from Montgomery College and a Graphic Design certificate from Shillington in New York, and I work in Adobe Illustrator, InDesign, and Photoshop.
